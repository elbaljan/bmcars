The Camino is just the beginning, it leads beyond.

The Camino doesn't end when you reach the hostel. BMCars is a local company that rents cars by the hour, at reduced prices, so you can explore without limits. How do our services work?

BMCars is a local, family-run company, which is why we prefer to manage the car rentals directly. Below, we explain the steps so you can understand how our process works.

Reserve

You can fill out the form or call us directly. However, the service will only be confirmed once we confirm your reservation.

Pick Up

We will bring the car to any location in the towns of Soutomaior or Pontesampaio, at no extra cost.

Drive

Enjoy the car for the agreed time and discover places you never imagined visiting. Unlimited mileage.

Return

Return it at the agreed location, hassle-free.
